The problem

05

Privacy

Everything lives on this phone.

No account, no sign-in, no Laytr server — because there isn’t one. Nothing you save is uploaded, scanned or sold. Turn on Airplane Mode and Laytr works the same.

Read the full privacy policy

What never leaves your device

  • Everything you save
  • The text Laytr reads out of your screenshots
  • Your notes, tags, cases and favourites
  • Every search you type

Laytr fetches the pages you saved, and nothing else. No advertising SDK, no tracking, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt — there is nothing to track you with.
